Keystream instrument recordings

Every instrument in the Sounds menu is one of these recordings.
The metronome click is a short tone generated in the browser.

Grand piano
  Salamander Grand Piano V3
  Recorded by Alexander Holm (Yamaha C5)
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

  Keystream ships a mono subset: velocity layers v2, v6, v11, and v16,
  with the long tails shortened, plus the hammer-release noises.
  Pitch zones follow the original instrument map.
